# Production Philosophy

AI filmmaking is a controlled production system, not prompt roulette. For commercial/creator work, **market before production**: first establish audience, click reason, hook, payoff, follow reason, repeatable series engine, monetization hypothesis, cheapest MVP, and AI-production fit. A beautiful internal pilot is not proof that a content business exists.

Separate decisions by lifetime: content-market hypotheses belong in the market gate, creative intent in the brief, persistent identity in asset passports, shot decisions in canonical specs, provider quirks in adapters, current facts in runtime artifacts, and verified lessons in model profiles.

Favor observable, reviewable states over adjectives. “Oppressive” is useful only after it becomes visible cues; “consistent” is useful only after the invariant is named. Design the smallest generative unit that can succeed independently and keep downstream work replaceable.

A production artifact earns promotion by passing a gate. Expensive generation never substitutes for missing content-market evidence or missing design work. Post-production owns tasks deterministic tools can execute more reliably, and editing may select a valid contiguous range rather than demanding every generated source second be perfect.
