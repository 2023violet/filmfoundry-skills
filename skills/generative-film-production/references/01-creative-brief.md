# Creative Brief

For audience-growth, monetization, or repeatable-series work, load `20-content-market-gate.md` first. The Creative Brief starts after the concept is at least eligible for MVP/production; it must not be used to disguise missing market answers with more worldbuilding.

Capture only decisions that change production: audience, format/platform hypothesis, duration range, purpose, Point X (what the viewer should understand/feel/do), core message/story, emotional arc, delivery specs, and hard constraints.

## Gate

Mark `BRIEF_LOCKED` when the audience, outcome, format, and non-negotiable constraints are explicit enough that two producers would not plan different projects from the same brief.

Use `../templates/creative-brief.md`.
