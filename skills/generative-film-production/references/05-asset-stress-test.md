# Asset Stress Test

Before expensive recurring video work, challenge the asset with low-cost views that expose drift: close-up front, three-quarter, profile, medium, full body/object, rear view when relevant, harsh light, low light, wide environment, and multi-subject interaction when the production needs it.

Reject identity-critical packs on catastrophic drift even if the average looks acceptable. Record which conditions fail; either improve the passport/reference pack or avoid those conditions in shot design.
