# Shot Engineering

Design the shot before compiling language. Required thinking is physical: who is where, what changes, where the camera is, what lens relationship is intended, how the camera moves through space, what the stage must visibly land on, and which consequences persist.

Prefer one dominant action. Composite camera motion is acceptable only when it behaves as one coherent move. When continuity matters, include axis, screen direction, eyeline, and prop state.

Use `../templates/shot-card.md` and validate structured JSON with `../scripts/validate_shot_spec.py`.
