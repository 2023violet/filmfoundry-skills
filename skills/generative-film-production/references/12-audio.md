# Audio

Treat dialogue, voice-over, ambience, foley/SFX, music, and captions as separate reviewable layers even when a video model can synthesize native audio. Lock verbatim dialogue before lip-sync work. Use second-level timing when audio creates a hard clock.

Native audio is a generation capability, not a substitute for final mix decisions. Review language, speaker identity, intelligibility, sync, ambience continuity, and music edit separately.
