# QC

Review in this order and stop at the first expensive failure:

1. Identity and subject count
2. Locks / reference roles
3. Stage end states
4. Anatomy, hands, props, product form
5. Spatial continuity, axis, eyeline
6. Camera and motion quality
7. Seam/pacing in playback
8. Audio source, language, sync
9. Editability and handles

Stills can validate identity, composition, texture, and some end states. They cannot establish motion rhythm, seam smoothness, pacing, or audio sync.

Use `../templates/qc-report.md`.
