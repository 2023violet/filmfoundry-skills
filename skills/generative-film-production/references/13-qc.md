# QC

Do not ask “is it beautiful?” first. Review production correctness before aesthetics.

Recommended order:

1. identity and subject count;
2. character/state/wardrobe and expensive locks;
3. narrative target and **eyeline** when relevant;
4. spatial persistence / geography / prop persistence;
5. observable end states and state transitions;
6. anatomy, hands, props, product form;
7. axis, screen direction, location continuity;
8. camera and motion quality;
9. acting discipline;
10. seam/pacing in full playback;
11. audio source, language, sync;
12. editability and handles;
13. visual quality / aesthetics.

Score separately where useful: **Prompt Compliance**, **Narrative Fitness**, **Identity/Continuity**, **Visual Quality**, and **Editability**. A visually excellent clip can still fail production compliance.

Before `RETRY`, inspect whether a contiguous range meets the shot goal. Use `PARTIAL_SELECT` when the valid range is sufficient and the failure lies outside it. A partial Select must remain traceable and downstream state must use its selected out-point.

Stills can validate identity, composition, texture, some eyelines, and some end states. They cannot establish motion rhythm, scene-reset timing, seam smoothness, pacing, acting amplification over time, or audio sync.

Use `../templates/qc-report.md`.
