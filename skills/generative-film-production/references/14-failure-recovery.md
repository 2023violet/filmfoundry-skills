# Failure Recovery

Diagnose before retrying. Convert the failed observation into the smallest causal hypothesis and change one related variable.

Examples: disappearing prop → make ownership/state persistent in the stage end state; identity swap → reduce competing references and strengthen role binding; over-cutting → remove timestamp density or conflicting camera commands; weak seam → use continuous tail relay only if the action is truly uninterrupted.

After several failures of the same class, change route or shot design. Do not hide structural generation failure behind endless sampling.
