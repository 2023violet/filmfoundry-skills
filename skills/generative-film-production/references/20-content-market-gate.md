# Content Market Gate

For audience-growth, monetization, or repeatable-series work, **market before production**. Do not spend on a full Production Bible, recurring Character Masters, large asset packs, or twenty-shot generation until the concept can answer the market questions and survive a cheap MVP.

This gate is about content demand, not provider prompting.

## Required questions

Before MVP work, answer all of these as explicit hypotheses:

1. **One-sentence conflict:** what problem, contradiction, impossible event, desire, threat, or transformation drives the content?
2. **Who watches?** name a specific audience rather than “everyone.”
3. **Why click?** what visible or verbal promise makes the content worth opening now?
4. **What is the first-three-second hook?** show the abnormal event, conflict, consequence, or irresistible question before background exposition when the format allows.
5. **What does the viewer get by the end?** payoff may be a reveal, laugh, emotion, useful fantasy, shock, satisfaction, resolution, or escalation.
6. **Why follow the next episode?** define the repeatable curiosity/character/world engine, not merely “another unrelated story.”
7. **Can the engine sustain thirty episodes?** list the rule that generates new conflicts without copying the same beat.
8. **What can eventually monetize?** platform revenue, sponsorship, licensing, IP, commerce, paid access, service, or another explicit route. If this is unknown, classify the work as a traffic experiment.
9. **What is the cheapest MVP?** smallest set of publishable videos that tests the content hypothesis without production-scale canon work.
10. **Why is it a fit for current AI-video production?** identify cast, locations, dialogue/lip-sync, action, hand/prop, and continuity burdens.
11. **Which platform/audience hypothesis is tested first?** platform rules change; research current rules at execution time rather than hard-coding them into FilmFoundry.

## Decisions

- `NO_GO` — one or more core content answers are missing or the economics/production burden is unjustified.
- `TRAFFIC_EXPERIMENT` — content hypothesis is complete but no monetization route is yet known.
- `MVP_ONLY` — content and commercial hypotheses are coherent; run the cheapest real publishing test before scale.
- `PRODUCTION_APPROVED` — real MVP evidence passed criteria declared before publication.
- `BYPASS` — explicit non-market route such as a client-locked commission, pure art piece, portfolio study, or model-capability test.

A polished internal demo is not market evidence. Likes from collaborators are not market evidence. Generated clips that “look good” are not market evidence.

## Evidence discipline

Declare project-specific success criteria before publishing the MVP. Capture dated evidence IDs and results. Do not hard-code universal retention or payout thresholds in the skill because platforms, niches, formats, and account maturity differ.
