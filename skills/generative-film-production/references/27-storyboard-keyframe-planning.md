# 27 · Storyboard and Keyframe Planning

## Storyboard is not a mandatory gate

Use storyboards when several states or camera choices need to be reviewed together. Use a single keyframe when one start-state decision is enough. Use first/last frames when the model must bridge two authoritative physical states.

## Panel grammar

Every planning panel should have one dominant visual fact:
- spatial establishment;
- action state;
- evidence/reveal;
- reaction/handoff.

## Director questions

For every panel/anchor answer:
- what must the viewer look at?
- what new information becomes visible?
- what is the subject’s screen direction and eyeline?
- what state must persist into the next cut?
- is this panel a provider input, edit reference, or review-only artifact?

## Important

Never derive shot count or runtime by multiplying storyboard grids. Final shot count is an editorial decision derived from story, timing authority, and usable Selects.
